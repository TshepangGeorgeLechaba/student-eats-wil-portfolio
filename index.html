Project Overview

Student Eats is a student-focused digital marketplace concept designed to connect student food vendors with students looking for affordable meals. The project brings together multimedia design, user experience, web development, Agile project coordination and project documentation.

This portfolio presents the project's supporting documents, planning evidence and reflection on the CPUT graduate attributes.
