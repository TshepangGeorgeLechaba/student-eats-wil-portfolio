About the Project

Student Eats is a student-focused digital marketplace concept designed to connect student food vendors with students looking for affordable meals. The project explores meal discovery, food listings and an organised ordering experience.

This portfolio presents the team's Agile documentation, project planning, graduate attribute reflection and supporting evidence.
